Guidance for using live SDR, schedules and station identification responsibly.

Uncertain identification and reception

Predicted signal quality, transmitter and program schedules, station names and suggested tuning frequencies are estimates or third-party information, not a promise of reception. Strong signals can be unidentified or misidentified.

Receiver-owner authority

Remote receivers are owned and administered independently. Each owner's channel limits, connectivity rules, region restrictions and terms prevail. FreqBeacon must identify as an external client and honor denials without retrying through alternate identities, paths or mirrors.

Listening rights

Available receiver audio is not automatically licensed for recording, redistribution, commercial exploitation or public rebroadcast. Broadcasters, rights holders and local laws may restrict such uses; consult relevant rules before doing so.

No regulatory endorsement

FreqBeacon is an independent radio discovery tool, not an official regulator, broadcaster, receiver operator, or emergency communications service. Do not rely on the application for emergency traffic, legally required monitoring or critical communications.

Source transparency

Review the Data sources & licensing page and repository SOURCE-COMPLIANCE.md for supported sources, explicit permissions, disabled integrations and attribution obligations. This legal draft grants no permissions from third parties.
